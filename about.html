About Our Organization

Founded on the principles of community responsibility, empathy, and sustainable outreach, the Community Action Project has grown from a small group of neighbors into a thriving network of organizers and advocates.

Our Vision

We envision a community where every individual has access to support, safety networks, and opportunities to thrive. By breaking down barriers between local resources and the people who need them, we work daily to turn this vision into reality.

Our History

What started as a simple weekend volunteer group has evolved to meet the changing dynamics of our region. Today, we manage multiple structured initiatives focused on direct local assistance and animal advocacy.
